About this survey

The Bangladesh Governance Trust Survey 2026 is part of OpenDataBD's annual citizen feedback initiative. It covers five thematic areas: institutional trust, local government service delivery, anti-corruption perceptions, digital public services, and justice & rule of law. Results will be published as an open dataset under a CC BY 4.0 licence once the survey closes.
