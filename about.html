OUR STORY

Building Bangladesh's
Data Commons

OpenDataBD was founded on a simple belief: transparent, accessible data is the foundation of a stronger, fairer Bangladesh.

MISSION

Why we exist

Bangladesh generates vast amounts of data from health outcomes in rural upazilas to flood records across river basins. Yet much of it remains locked in departmental silos, inaccessible to researchers, journalists, and citizens who need it most.

OpenDataBD exists to change that. We curate, verify, and publish public datasets under open licenses so anyone can explore, download, and build with them for free.

PRINCIPLES

What guides us

balance

Civic Accountability

Public data belongs to the public. We publish government and institutional datasets that citizens have a right to access and scrutinise.

science

Evidence-Based Policy

Better data leads to better decisions. We work with researchers and institutions to surface datasets that can directly inform public policy.

groups

Inclusive Access

We design for low-bandwidth conditions and offer data in multiple formats (CSV, JSON, XML) so even resource-constrained teams can participate.
