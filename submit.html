Submit a Dataset

Share a Bangladesh dataset with the open data community. All submissions are reviewed before publishing (usually within 24–48 hours).

Datasets must relate to Bangladesh, be from a credible source, and be legally shareable under an open license. Government, NGO, research institution, and UN agency data are all welcome.
